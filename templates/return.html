⚖️ Return & Fine Policy
  • Returning a copy resets its status back to Available.
  • Late returns trigger automatic creation of a FINE entity record.
  • Fine Rate: ₹5.00 per day overdue beyond the scheduled DueDate.
  • As specified in the ER diagram, fines are managed and recorded under a LIBRARIAN (relationship: LIBRARIAN MANAGES FINE).
